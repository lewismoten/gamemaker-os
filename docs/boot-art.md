# Boot art

Approved art from ChatGPT GPT-6.1 Sol, made from the maintainer's directions (MIT, `art/LICENSE.txt`); the layout notes are `art/README.txt`.

- **Splash** (`ba_splash`, `src/bootart.c`): 960x540 RGB565, drawn right after the OS sets that mode (the kernel starts a program in 640x480x32, so the 640x480 art is not used) and shown until the games list is ready. It keeps the surface's stride, not the picture width.
- **Library diagnostics** (`ba_diag`): shown instead of an empty list when no game is found. The OS prints its reasons in the framed area, 24 px a row, up to 12 rows.
- **Loading animation:** a yellow comet travels along eight segments (12 px, 2 px gaps, x 425 to 534) once every 1.6 s, with a tail of two segments either side. A segment rests light blue; at the comet's edge it switches to a gold (#B79C39, yellow #F8D450 dimmed to the relative luminance of the blue, a hair above it in RGB565), so the switch has no brightness change, then brightens to yellow at the peak and back. No segment is ever darker than the resting one (a host test checks every frame by relative luminance). `BA_RAMP` 1 rests on the gold instead. 16 shades, all exact RGB565. The clock picks the phase when the scan's heartbeat checkpoints call, so uneven checkpoints skip shades instead of slowing it; at most one redraw of the bar strip per 50 ms, one last step before the list, no delay added. The log prints `GMOS splash longest gap between checkpoints` (92 ms warm, 109 ms cold on the board). The scan cache is no longer written while the splash shows: `cache_end` only closes it in memory and `cache_flush` persists it once the list is on screen. A cold write of 107 KB took: open 0 ms, 27 small writes 18 ms in all (worst under 1 ms), fsync 423 ms, close and rename 809 ms, so the time is the SD card's indivisible commit, not the writes; the list is up and key presses queue in the kernel meanwhile (once, on a cold run). `BA_SEGS` 4 gives four wide bars.
- **Data:** `tools/make_bootart.py` turns `art/badge-96.png` into `src/bootart_data.c`: 13 RGB565 colors and 1565 bytes of run-length data (high nibble color, low nibble run - 1). No PNG decoder runs on the board. Text is the OS's 5x7 font at 6 x scale a letter. The badge is drawn at 2x on the splash and 80 px (nearest neighbor) on the diagnostics screen. Together it costs about 4 KB of the 8 MiB image quota.
- **Tests:** `tests/test_bootart.py` renders both screens to `build/host/bootart/` (set `GMOS_SHOTS` to copy them elsewhere) and compares them with `art/boot-960x540.png` and `art/diag-960x540.png`; a few dozen pixels differ where the art's glyphs and the OS font disagree by a pixel.
- **E-ink status is linked, hardware-untested.** `src/eink.c` draws the 250x122 loading page and a 40 px castle from the boot badge at runtime, then shows library count, game title and F5 `SAVING` status using optional ABI 1.9. See [status behavior and retry limits](build.md#optional-e-ink-status-abi-19). `art/eink/` keeps the reference PBMs and PNGs; these are not embedded as full-page assets.
- The [Pages site](index.html) uses `docs/favicon.ico` and local images and fonts.
