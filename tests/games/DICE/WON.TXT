THROUGH!

One die, two doors.

The die is a ring of six
blocks. Face 1 is set to turn
into face 2 after a few ticks
(18.2 a second), face 2 into
face 3, and so on round to 1
again. Each face block turns
into a still copy of itself
when touched, and gives a key
(a gold one for 1, 3, 5 and a
silver one for 2, 4, 6).

Game-Maker has no random
number. The clock starts when
the room loads, so the face
you stop on depends only on
the tick you touch it. Wait a
little longer or a little less
and the roll changes, but the
same walk always rolls the
same.

Open the game in Game-Maker,
pick a face block in the
Blocks tab and look at its
Next block and its time.
Change a number, then play
your change.
